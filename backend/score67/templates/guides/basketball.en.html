Basketball

Basketball live ticker: keep the score on your phone and share it live

At many youth games hardly anyone knows the exact score: the gym has no scoreboard or it only shows points, and parents outside get nothing at all. With Score67 one person courtside keeps the score on a phone – everyone else follows it live through a link, with no app and no sign-up.

What you record during the game

  • Points: 2 and 3 pointers, free throws made or missed – for a player or just the team.
  • Fouls: team fouls, personal fouls or both, technical fouls; players are flagged at 4 and 5 fouls.
  • Rebounds, timeouts and the possession arrow.
  • Players on court: tap players to put them on court or on the bench – for formats with 4 or 5 players.

The console runs in landscape: pick the action first (e.g. “2 points”), then tap the player. Mistakes are fixed with Undo, and entries are kept even on a weak connection in the gym.

Game clock and formats – youth games included

The clock counts down and is run with play and stop, like at the scorer's table. Presets cover 4 × 10, 4 × 12, 8 × 5 minutes (common for U10/U12) and 2 × 20; any other format can be set freely. If the score is tied after the last period, Score67 offers overtime. If you forget to start a period, the app asks before points land in the wrong quarter.

Box score: points, free throws and fouls per player

The “BS” tab shows minutes, points, free throws (made/attempted and percentage), 2s, 3s, fouls and rebounds per player – for each team, for the whole game or one period.

Share the live ticker

Send the link via WhatsApp, Facebook or your phone's share menu; the chat shows a preview with both teams and the score. Public games are also listed under live games; private games can only be seen with the link. At the final whistle you confirm the final result; the score and game log stay available.

Free and without an account

Score67 runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop. A scoreboard is set up in a minute; a six-digit PIN protects the Controller, and more devices can join as Controllers.
