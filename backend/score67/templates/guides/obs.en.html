Live streaming

Scoreboard overlay for OBS: show the score in your live stream

Streaming games on YouTube, Twitch or Facebook needs a score bug in the picture. Score67 provides a transparent overlay you add to OBS Studio (or Streamlabs) as a browser source. The score comes live from the phone courtside – no extra software on the streaming computer.

Set up in three steps

  1. Create a scoreboard and open the Sharing tab in the Controller. It has the OBS link (with its own key for private games).
  2. In OBS add a new Browser source, paste the link and set the size to 1920 × 1080.
  3. Done: every entry in the Controller appears in the stream, including the running clock.

Adjust without touching OBS

Under Game settings → Overlay in the Controller choose small, large or hidden, top left or top right. Optionally the overlay switches to the large view in breaks and at full time. If your stream runs with a delay, set the same delay so the score matches the picture.

For basketball and football

The overlay shows team short names, colours, points or goals, period or half and the clock; in football also cards. Team logos are uploaded when editing the game in “My scoreboards”. More on each sport: basketball live ticker and football live ticker.
