In the gym and at the pitch

Scoreboard from your phone: show the score on a TV, projector or LED display

Not every gym has a scoreboard, and football pitches usually have none at all. With Score67 any screen becomes one: the score is kept on a phone and shows up at the same time, large, on a TV, projector or tablet courtside.

TV, projector or tablet

Open the game's Viewer link in the browser of the device – a smart TV, a laptop on a projector or a tablet. The display adapts to the screen, shows teams, score, period and clock and updates by itself. Switch between the simple and the extended view (with scorers or points per player) in the menu.

LED displays over Bluetooth

In the Controller, Game settings → Displays connects up to two Bluetooth LED displays straight to the phone (Chrome on Android). Score67 sends the score – and, if you like, the time – to the display on every change.

Real scoreboards – in the works

Next up is connecting fixed scoreboards over Wi-Fi: a small device at the board is paired once with a code and then shows the selected scoreboard. How to build such a board yourself from an LED panel and an ESP32 is in the LED scoreboard build guide – parts list included. If you would like to test it, write to us in the forum.
