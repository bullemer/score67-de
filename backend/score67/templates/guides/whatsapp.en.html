How-to

Share the score live on WhatsApp – here's how

Instead of posting in the parents' group after every quarter, share one link – and everyone sees the score live until the final whistle. This is how it works with Score67:

  1. Create a scoreboard: on score67.de choose the sport, playing time and teams. No account needed.
  2. Share the link: in the Controller's Sharing tab tap WhatsApp and pick the group. Or copy the Viewer link.
  3. Play: tap points or goals into the Controller. Anyone who opens the link sees the current score straight away – no app, no sign-up, no reloading.

With a preview in the chat

WhatsApp shows a preview image with both teams and the score at the moment of sharing – so the chat shows straight away what it's about. The link itself always stays current.

Public or private

Public games also appear under live games. Switch on Private game in the Sharing tab and only people with the link can see it – handy for youth games.

After the game

At the final whistle you confirm the final result. The link then shows the final score with every period; in the live list the game stays under “Just finished” for a few hours.
