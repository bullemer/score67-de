Football

Live ticker for kids' and youth football – the score live on every phone

On weekends the youth teams often play at the same time, and anyone not at the pitch only learns the result in the evening from the parents' group. With Score67 a coach or parent taps goals and cards into a phone – everyone else sees the score, the match minute and the scorers live through a link.

Any format: 5, 7, 9 or 11 players

When you create the scoreboard you choose the playing time (e.g. 2 × 20, 2 × 25 or 2 × 45 minutes) and how many players are on the pitch at once – from small-sided games to 11-a-side. The squad is optional: shirt numbers are enough, names can be left out.

Goals, cards and the match minute

  • Goals with scorer, penalties included (scored or missed),
  • yellow and red cards, fouls and free kicks,
  • substitutions by tapping players,
  • the clock runs from each half's kick-off; stoppage time shows as 45+2′.

Live for parents, grandparents and the club

Share the link in the parents' WhatsApp group; the chat shows a preview with both teams and the current score. Anyone who opens the link sees the score without an app or sign-up – with scorers if they like. Public games appear under live games; with “Private game” the ticker stays within your group.

Privacy for children

Score67 needs no names: shirt numbers are enough for every feature. Only enter names if parents and children agree. There are no ads and no tracking – details in the privacy policy (German).
